Music Visualiser

- A p5.js version of Windows Media Player 11 Black Theme

How to Play

  1. The mouse can control play or pause and play speed.
    Click play button to play, and fast play button for playing faster, slow play button for playing slower
  2. Press "space bar" to play or stop the audio play, "m" to show or hide list of visual names, 1 - 5 digit buttons for selecting visual, left or right arrows to select on-going songs, "f" and "s" for playing faster or slower respectively, "g" for fullscreen
